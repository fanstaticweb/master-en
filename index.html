Welcome to The Haven

A peaceful refuge crafted for intentional living.

Discover an intimate escape designed to slow you down and reconnect you to the elements.

Located along the rugged coastline, The Haven merges architectural simplicity with warm, personalized B&B hospitality. Each corner is constructed with native stone, timber, and soft linens, creating an atmospheric retreat where your morning coffee is accompanied by nothing but bird songs and breaking waves.
